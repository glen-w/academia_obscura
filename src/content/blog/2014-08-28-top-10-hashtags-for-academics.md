If you are new to twitter, or even if you've been around a while, the wave of content can be overwhelming. To get the most out of twitter,

**#altac, #postac**
Not alternative academia, but alternatives to academia. This hashtag is used by those

**#AcademicHumor, #AcHum**
OK, so this one is a bit of self-promotion. We started tweeting some of our stuff with #AcHum, because writing 'academic humor' in full is a pain, and because ever since Webster removed a load of vowels from the English language it is hard to know whether it is best to write 'humor' or 'humour'. #AcHum solves this problem!

**#ScholarSunday**
Scholar Sunday is like #FollowFriday (usually shortened to #ff) for academics - use it to give a shout out to your favourite academics on twitter.

> [#ScholarSunday](https://twitter.com/hashtag/ScholarSunday?src=hash) started as an alternative to the boring [#FollowFriday](https://twitter.com/hashtag/FollowFriday?src=hash) - instead of just listing scholars' IDs, we explain WHY we follow them.

— Dr Raul Pacheco-Vega (@raulpacheco) [August 25, 2014](https://twitter.com/raulpacheco/statuses/503737989319892993)
