The Book

Academia Obscura: The Hidden Silly Side of Higher Education collects the stranger corners of scholarly life — baffling studies, accidental humour, and rituals that only make sense from inside a university.

It was crowdfunded with Unbound by oddball academics around the world, and published in late 2017 / 2018. “Write a book,” they said. “It’ll be fun,” they said. It mostly was.
